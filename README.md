The Banking Loans Dataset provides structured information on loan accounts, customer characteristics, loan terms, credit risk indicators, and repayment-related financial metrics. The dataset is designed to support exploratory data analysis, data cleaning, visualization, and the development of analytical insights within a banking and lending context.
The dataset contains 12,035 records and 20 columns, with monetary values expressed in Egyptian Pounds (EGP). Loan origination dates range from 2019 to 2025, allowing users to explore lending patterns and portfolio characteristics over time. Dataset Contents

The dataset covers several key areas of banking loan information:
Record and Loan Identification: Record identifiers and loan identifiers.
Customer Information: Customer age, city, branch, and employment status.
Financial Information: Annual income, loan amount, monthly installment, and remaining loan balance.
Loan Characteristics: Loan type, loan purpose, interest rate, and repayment term.
Credit Risk Indicators: Credit score, debt-to-income ratio, and risk grade.
Loan Administration: Loan origination date, application channel, and loan status.
Key Features

The dataset includes information that can be used to analyze:
Loan distribution across different cities and branches.
Loan types and purposes across the lending portfolio.
Relationships between customer income, loan amounts, and debt-to-income ratios.
Credit scores and risk-grade distributions.
Interest rates and loan repayment terms.
Loan statuses, including active, closed, defaulted, restructured, and written-off accounts.
Remaining loan balances and monthly installment patterns.
Loan origination trends across the available time period.
